# Pace

Pace is a desktop companion that compares your own Codex allowance with an even target from 100% at the start of a usage period to 0% at reset.

- Live remaining allowance and reset countdown.
- Target remaining now and percentage-point difference from your actual balance.
- Hourly pacing guidance and recorded history across allowance periods.
- Local storage separated by signed-in account; no usage upload to the publisher.

## Requirements and use

Requires a local Codex desktop session, Node.js 22 or newer, and Codex CLI signed in with your own ChatGPT account. Windows has been tested; macOS and Linux have not been verified. Phone and cloud synchronization are not included.

After installation, ask **Start Pace** to start the local tracker and open its dashboard. Readings update every minute while Pace and your computer are running. Closing the dashboard does not stop the tracker. Ask **Stop Pace** to stop recording; history remains saved. Tracking does not start automatically after a computer restart.

Ask **Am I on pace?** to compare your remaining allowance with the fixed target. Targets are calculated guidance, not a prediction of how many prompts a task will use. Missing history is never reconstructed.

Pace is being prepared for the OpenAI plugin directory. Directory approval and publication are not yet confirmed.
